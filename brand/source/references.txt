COLOR CONTRAST
W3C: Understanding WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.4.3, Contrast (Minimum)
https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
Normal text: minimum 4.5:1. Large text: minimum 3:1.
The four recommended text pairs in the guide meet at least 4.5:1.
Ratios are calculated from the packet's sRGB values, not sampled from images.
This is a text-pair check, not a claim that an entire implementation meets WCAG.
